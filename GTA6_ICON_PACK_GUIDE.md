# GTA VI / Vice City (GTX 6) Stream Deck Icon Pack Guide

A complete, high-definition icon pack and telemetry suite designed for Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6 / Vice City) gameplay, FiveM roleplay, and live broadcast streaming on Twitch, YouTube, and Kick.

---

## 1. Visual Theme Identity and Palette

Inspired by the official Grand Theft Auto VI visual reveal, the icon pack adopts a retro-modern Vice City synthwave aesthetic featuring vibrant twilight sunsets, neon luminescence, and silhouette palm fronds.

### Primary Color Palette
* **Deep Vice Twilight Indigo**: `#1A0A26` to `#2D124D` (Background slate and dark bevels)
* **Neon Hot Magenta / Pink**: `#D82276` to `#FF1493` (Inner neon glow and highlights)
* **Sunset Peach / Gold**: `#FFA044` to `#FFB800` (Horizon heat and secondary accents)
* **Electric Cyan / Aqua**: `#00F0FF` (Reticles, audio meters, and data indicators)
* **Pure Crisp White**: `#FFFFFF` (Vector glyphs and high-contrast typography)

### File Format & Resolution Standards
* **Physical Keycaps**: `186 x 186 px` (Lossless PNG with rounded bevels and labels)
* **High-DPI Keycaps (2x)**: `372 x 372 px` (Enhanced with Lanczos super-sampling)
* **Isolated Transparent Glyphs**: `186 x 186 px` (32-bit RGBA with smooth alpha transparency for OBS overlays, Touch Portal, and Loupedeck)
* **Telemetry Widget Background**: `458 x 196 px` (Exact dimensions for CPU, RAM, GPU dynamic system monitors)

## 6. Customization and Automation Workflow

1. **Adding to Elgato Stream Deck Software**:
   * Open the Elgato Stream Deck application.
   * Drag your desired action (e.g. Hotkey, OBS Scene, Soundboard) onto an empty key.
   * Click the icon thumbnail in the property inspector and select **Set from File**.
   * Navigate to `output/themes/gta6_vice/gaming_icons/keycaps/` or `streaming_icons/keycaps/` and select your icon.
2. **Using Transparent Glyphs**:
   * Transparent PNGs in `transparent_glyphs/` can be layered over animated video backgrounds, custom OBS overlays, Touch Portal buttons, or stream alerts.
3. **Re-generating or Modifying Palette**:
   * To adjust colors, labels, or sizes, modify `generate_gta6_icon_pack.py` and run:
   ```powershell
   python generate_gta6_icon_pack.py
   ```
