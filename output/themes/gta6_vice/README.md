# GTA VI / Vice City (GTX 6) Pro Stream Deck Icon Suite Guide

A high-definition, large-proportion icon suite and telemetry package designed specifically for **Ulanzi**, **Elgato Stream Deck (MK.2 & XL)**, and **Touch Portal** hardware controllers, covering both GTA VI gaming operations and live OBS broadcast streaming.

---

## 1. Visual Theme Identity & Proportions

Based on user feedback and hardware ergonomics from real-world Ulanzi and OBS Control Room setups, this edition features **bold, large-scale icon proportions**:

* **Hero Glyph Scale**: The central symbol occupies **55% to 70%** of the total keycap area (75px to 135px tall/wide inside the 186x186 px button), ensuring crisp visibility from 2 to 3 feet away during gaming sessions.
* **Line Weight**: Heavy **4px to 8px bold line weights** and solid geometry replacing thin strokes.
* **Neon Luminescence**: Luminous dual-tone ambient drop-glow in **Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`)**, **Hot Magenta (`#D82276`)**, and **Sunset Gold (`#FFA044`)**.
* **Base Keycap**: Deep Vice Violet dusk gradient (`#160824` to `#2B0C3C`) with subtle palm silhouettes and beveled neon borders.
